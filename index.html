01 — Thesis

AI expertise is a product job, not a model job.

Most teams start with a model.

I start with a decision: who is stuck, what judgment is expensive, and whether intelligence will change the outcome. If it will not, we do not build it.

The demo is the cheapest lie in AI.

A chatbot that answers once is theatre. An AI product has retrieval, guardrails, cost, evals, and a human who still owns the last mile.

The highest-leverage AI is unfairness, inverted.

I published research on using AI to personalize learning in India — because a one-size-fits-all system clones privilege. That is the same instinct I bring to enterprise products.

02 — The AI PM craft

How I take intelligence from a slide to a system.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Opportunity canvas. Feasibility. When not to use AI. Kill criteria before the first token is spent.

  2. 02

    Spec

    PRDs written for GenAI, RAG, and agents — jobs to be done, failure modes, and the metric that will fire someone.

  3. 03

    Orchestrate

    Tools, handoffs, memory, MCP. The product is the graph, not the prompt.

  5. 05

    Ship

    Deploy, teach the team, mentor the market. A live URL beats a strategy memo.

04 — Signature research

AI should close gaps, not clone privilege.

IJRSI · Volume XI, Issue IV · 2024 · DOI 10.51244/IJRSI.2024.1104066

AI For Bridging Socio-Economic Inequities in Indian Education Space

Arijit Goswami & Akshay Sharma · Product Management, Brane Enterprises

India runs one of the world’s largest school systems — and a one-size-fits-all pedagogy. Our paper argued that AI earns the right to exist in that system only if it personalizes attention: adaptive learning, bias-light assessment, and teacher time given back to the child who would otherwise disappear in the average.

We used UDISE, World Bank, NITI Aayog, and UNICEF data, plus conversations with educators and policymakers. A related background paper was acknowledged in UNESCO’s State of Education Report for India 2022.

The product lesson I still use: if the model cannot see the student at the edge of the system, it is not intelligence. It is a mirror of the already-served.

ResearchGate Journal page

What the paper refuses

  • AI as a substitute for teachers
  • Demos that never reach a government school
  • Average metrics that hide caste, gender, and income gaps

What it asks for

  • Personalized pedagogy at national scale
  • Automated grading that frees teacher time
  • Value cases a policymaker can fund
